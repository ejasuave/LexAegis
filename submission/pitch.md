## Script

**Problem (about 25 s).** Before someone signs a settlement agreement, an adviser checks it, often with
AI help. Afterwards nobody can show what exactly was approved: the file, the figures, or by whom. A
mismatch like a total that does not equal its instalments can go unnoticed, and the approval lives in a
database the recipient has to trust.

**What we built (about 30 s).** Proof of Review is a small open protocol: a person approves an exact
package, a registry on Monad records only a commitment, and anyone can check the same package without
the app. LexAegis, for employment settlements in England and Wales, is the first reference application.
The model proposes; code and the human decide.

**Why Monad (about 10 s).** Fast, cheap finality makes a per-approval receipt practical.

**What exists today (about 25 s).** The registry is deployed on Monad Testnet and matches its source. A
genuine qwen3.8-max run finds the gap in a synthetic agreement. An independent verifier and SDK read
receipts. Hosted use and real-device passkey evidence are not done yet.

**Next (about 10 s).** Put a real adviser's workflow through it and find out what they would need to
trust a receipt.
