# Adoption roadmap: 30, 60 and 90 days

> **Plan, not commitment.** Every step is an intention with a test for whether to continue, change or
> stop. A target is not completed traction. Nothing below is built, scheduled or promised; no external
> trial, customer, design partner or integration exists today (see [`traction-log.md`](./traction-log.md),
> currently empty). A calendar date is not authorisation to deploy anything.

**Day 0:** the first working day after the hackathon result is known. **Start date: NOT YET SET** (it
depends on the result and on the owner's decision). The phases are measured from Day 0, never from a
date invented here. The hard submission deadline is 2026-10-14T04:59+01:00 and is outside this plan.

This is the one canonical adoption roadmap. [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) points here and holds no
separate commitments. A step that needs evidence records it through the
[traction log](./traction-log.md), the [integration trials](../integration-trials.md) or a dated note.

Standing rules for every phase:

- **No client-data pilot** before separate, explicit operational and provider-data decisions. Synthetic
  and fictional material only until then.
- Additional review profiles are **demand-led**: a profile is built because an actual adopter asked for
  it, with the generic and legal boundaries preserved.
- Version 1 **discloses the full supplied package**. Anything else is research (see Days 61 to 90).

## What would change this plan

A conversation or trial that contradicts the assumption that a portable, independently checkable record
helps a real handover is a result. If the evidence says no, the right step is to say so and stop, not
to widen the claim. Counts that support a pitch come only from the evidence-reviewed external rows in
the traction log.
