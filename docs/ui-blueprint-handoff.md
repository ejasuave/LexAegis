## Definition of done

Passed means a check above exercised it. Not run means no evidence exists yet.

**Visual and layout.** One continuous canvas environment with no opaque cards: passed (rails, stage and notices are hairline groups; only the palette and the approval dialog have a dark floor). Command-mode nucleus with calm periphery: passed in screenshots. Review mode keeps the document first and its scroll per mode: implemented; scroll restoration not measured. Text readable in idle, running, alert and confirmation scenes: passed for contrast of the art under reading regions (channel at most 0 against the 0x17 limit in all 12 fixtures); not verified by a person on a physical screen. Ceiling flush with unclipped focus: passed at six widths. Critical amounts, signing actions and Stop reachable at every width: Stop 44 px and in every modal, passed; 320 px layout passed without page-wide horizontal scroll.

**Truth and authorisation.** No hardcoded activity: fixtures are gated and absent from a normal run. Particles only for a running tool: passed. Alert branches end at real visible findings or the labelled control: passed on fixtures only. Duplicate, out-of-order and obsolete events: unit-tested (`RunEventGate`, the assistant controller's own rules). Envio, Mera, vault and transaction stay separate readings: passed. Pending, reverted, failed and rejected signing never celebrate: unit-tested. One live confirmation gives at most one effect and refresh, replay and remount give none: passed (controller unit tests, `hud-receipt-check`). Consent, save and exact approval boundaries unchanged: the existing 73 and 61 check journeys pass. No secret or plaintext in telemetry: the privacy journey passes.

**Interaction and accessibility.** Keyboard-only path through matter, citation, command, Stop and the approval ceremony: covered in pieces (command check, journeys); no single keyboard-only run. Palette focus, containment and restoration: passed (p95 27 ms to focus on a development machine). Stop reachable with palette or approval open: passed. Reduced motion, Effects Off and forced colours: passed. 320 px, 200% text and 400% zoom: 320 px passed; **200% text and 400% zoom not run by hand**. Screen-reader announcements: structure present (one polite announcement per accepted event); **not tested with a screen reader**. Pointer targets, focus visibility and real flash behaviour: Stop and controls at least 44 px, focus not hidden behind the ceiling or dock, passed; **flash safety was not assessed with a luminance tool**. The wash peaks at 0.06 opacity (channel 15) and fades by 600 ms, which is a design bound, not proof.

**Resilience and performance.** Canvas failure leaves a usable app: construction failure falls back to the SVG marker (code path present, not forced in a test other than Effects Off). Hidden tab and Off stop rendering: passed. Resize and effects churn leave one canvas and one loop: passed. Quality adaptation: unit-tested (steps down only). **Performance budgets are not met by evidence**: canvas work p95 about 0.4 to 1.1 ms was measured on this development machine only; there was no mid-range integrated-GPU desktop or mobile device, no long-task trace and no hardware record. Fixtures cover idle, queued, awaiting, running, concurrent alert, stopping, offline, stale, locked, pending, failed and confirmed; the corrected confirmation is covered by fixture buttons.

## Remaining blockers and known gaps

- Run the four failing journeys' environment problem separately (server sign-in), then re-check review and history on this UI.
- Record the performance trace and flash review on real hardware; do the 200% text, 400% zoom and screen-reader passes.
- The mask can lag a few frames behind a layout change that grows a reading region; the 24 px margin covered the tested cases only.
- Alerts were verified on synthetic findings; a live run with real findings was not driven through the canvas.
- The matter rail is a labelled disclosure (not a modal drawer) between 800 and 1199 px.
- A long silent model call can read as "cannot be confirmed" after 60 s because SSE heartbeats are deliberately not progress.
- The Chapter 10 evidence images and record were left unchanged. The pre-redesign journey screenshots there no longer match the UI.
